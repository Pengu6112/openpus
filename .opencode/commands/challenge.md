description: Attack a plan, file, or project and say what's wrong with it
---

Target: $ARGUMENTS — if empty, the change or plan we just discussed.

Argue against it. One line naming what you are attacking, then:

- Should it exist at all? What is unnecessary, over-built, or solving a problem we do not have.
- What breaks if we are wrong, and how we would find out.
- The simpler or cheaper option we skipped, and why it might be better.
- What evidence would change the verdict.

Be specific: cite the file, line, or assumption you are attacking. If it is sound, say so in one line and name its weakest part. Change no code.
